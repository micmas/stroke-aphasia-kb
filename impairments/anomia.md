# Anomia

Word-finding and naming impairment: the symptom that every aphasia
syndrome shows to some degree and that most lesion-symptom mapping,
recovery and therapy studies measure directly (naming accuracy,
naming latency, word retrieval in discourse). It is distinct from
`anomic_aphasia`, the classified syndrome in which anomia is the
predominant deficit with relatively preserved fluency, comprehension
and repetition.

Findings target this entry when the outcome is a naming score, whatever
the sample's syndrome labels were. The therapy literature on naming
(semantic feature analysis, phonological component analysis, cueing
hierarchies, tDCS with naming practice) targets it too; the
`therapy` block on each finding says which intervention.
